card page in an article charts graphs

The eleven graph families the layout is held to, each one case of the bench fixture graph-families, so every figure is a real graph result drawn by the runtime. Point at a node to see its edges, or tab to a picture and follow the edges with the arrow keys. Every picture also lists its edges as a table.

The graphs are the layout's test data, in packages/runtime/test/graph/families.ts: the Node tests hold every one to no overlaps, a ceiling on crossings, centred ports and 50 ms.
