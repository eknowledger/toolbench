### New (5)

- **Reset button**: puts a tool's inputs back where they started.
- **Share a link**: a link icon copies the page address with the tool's inputs in it. _On by default_
- **Events**: your page hears when a tool's inputs change and when a run finishes.
- **Links under a tool**: show them all, only "Open the full tool", or none.
- **Chart settings**: one setting for every chart's text size, one for its maximum width.

### Fixed (3)

- **Readable chart labels**: chart labels are the same size on a phone as on a wide screen.
- **Graphs in closed cards**: a graph in a closed card is drawn when it opens, instead of showing an error.
- **Smaller first load**: the runtime's stylesheets no longer ship their comments.
