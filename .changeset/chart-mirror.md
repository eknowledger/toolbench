**New: mirrored horizontal bars, for pyramids and back-to-back comparisons.** ([#125](https://github.com/eknowledger/toolbench/issues/125)) `mirror: true` on a horizontal chart draws negative values to the left and labels every number, on the axis, in the readout and in the table, as its magnitude.
