**New: every kind of graph.** ([#152](https://github.com/eknowledger/toolbench/issues/152)) Beyond layered graphs, a `graph` can be a tidy tree, a radial mind map, a force-laid network or nodes placed by hand, with straight, curved, spline or polyline edges. It can draw hexagons, parallelograms, cylinders and documents, record nodes whose rows each have a port, crow's-foot and UML markers, nested groups a reader can close and open, and swimlanes. Each of these is a small chunk fetched by the first graph that uses it, so a page pays only for the graphs it draws.
