**New: scatter plots.** ([#120](https://github.com/eknowledger/toolbench/issues/120)) A series can carry its own `x` values, so measurements that share no x positions plot together. The readout snaps to the nearest point, with both crosshair lines, and the keyboard steps through the points left to right; the data table lists one row per point.
