**New: radar charts.** ([#128](https://github.com/eknowledger/toolbench/issues/128)) A `radar` output draws a profile across several measures, one spoke each from 12 o'clock, on a shared scale from zero or each axis's own maximum, with round-valued rings, markers on every vertex and a readout that steps spoke by spoke.
