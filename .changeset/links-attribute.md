**New: choose the links under a tool.** `links="page"` shows only "Open the full tool" and `links="none"` shows nothing, for a tool in an article that lists its references already. The default, `all`, keeps the manifest's links. Also a property: `host.links = "none"`. ([#216](https://github.com/eknowledger/toolbench/issues/216))

A retired tool keeps its links whatever this says, because its notice points at them.
