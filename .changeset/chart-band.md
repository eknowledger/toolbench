**New: bands.** ([#119](https://github.com/eknowledger/toolbench/issues/119)) `shape: "band"` with a `lower` edge fills the range between two edges, a p5 to p95 spread around a median say, beneath every other series. The readout reads it as "29 to 35 ms", and the data table shows both edges.
